GLINT-862 repro: Ctrl+, after submit should open Settings immediately.

Steps:
1. Open the app and focus the submit composer.
2. Type a message and press Submit (or Enter).
3. Immediately press Ctrl+, (Cmd+, on macOS).

Expected: Settings opens right away, even though the composer still has focus.
Fix: register the shortcut on window in capture phase so focused inputs cannot swallow it.
